Web App Project

Personal Interview CRM

Track job applications and interview experiences — privately.

⚙ Coming Soon — Not yet publicly deployed

Personal Interview CRM is a personal productivity application for managing your job search. Record applications, log interview rounds, capture the questions you were asked, and keep your own answers and improved responses — all in a private, account-linked workspace. Sign in with your Google account to get started.

What You Can Do

Features available in Personal Interview CRM

Track Job Applications

Create a record for each position you apply to — company name, role, application date, current status, and priority level.

Save Job Descriptions

Keep the full job posting saved alongside each application for easy reference throughout the interview process.

Update Status & Priority

Mark applications as active, in-progress, rejected, or on hold. Set priority levels to stay focused on what matters most.

Record Interview Rounds

Log each interview stage separately — phone screens, technical rounds, and final panels — linked to the application.

Store Interview Q&A

Record the questions you were asked in each interview round and the answers you gave, linked to the specific stage.

Record Improved Answers

Note better answers alongside your original responses to build a personal bank of prepared interview answers over time.

Organize Interview Experiences

Keep all your interview preparation, feedback, and notes in one structured, searchable private workspace.

Private Account Workspace

Sign in with Google to get a personal workspace. Your records are linked to your account only — other users cannot see your data.

Authentication & Privacy

How Google Sign-In works in Personal Interview CRM

What Google Sign-In does

  • ✓ Authenticates you using your existing Google account
  • ✓ Shares your account's unique identifier with the application
  • ✓ Provides your email address for account display
  • ✓ Provides your display name for the interface
  • ✓ Uses only openid, email, and profile scopes

What the application does not access

  • ✗ Your Google account password
  • ✗ Gmail messages or inbox
  • ✗ Google Drive files
  • ✗ Google Calendar events
  • ✗ Google Contacts
  • ✗ Any other Google service data

Personal Interview CRM uses Google OpenID Connect and requests only basic identity information.
See the Privacy Policy for a complete description of how your data is handled.

Your Data, Your Workspace

Your records stay private and associated only with your account

Account-Linked Records

Every application, interview round, and note you create is linked to your account identifier. Other users cannot view or access your records.

No Data Sharing

Your job applications and interview notes are private. The application does not share your records with other users or third-party services.

No Extra Password

Sign in using your existing Google account. No separate password for this application is created or stored.
